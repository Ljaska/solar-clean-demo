What builds up

Clean glass lets your system do its work.

Solar panels sit outside all year. Even when they look clean from the ground, a thin layer can remain on the glass and collect around frames.

01

Dust

Dry weather and nearby roads leave a fine film that ordinary rainfall may only redistribute.

02

Bird droppings

Localised deposits can bond to warm glass and create persistent shaded areas.

03

Pollen

Fine pollen becomes tacky when wet and often settles along the lower edges of an array.

04

Leaves

Leaves and organic debris retain moisture, block drainage and create shaded patches.

05

Lichen

Slow-growing organic matter needs patient, panel-safe removal—not scraping or harsh chemicals.

06

Air pollution

Traffic and industrial particles can combine with moisture to form a stubborn surface residue.

Performance loss

Soiling loss is real—but every site is different.

Contamination reduces the light reaching solar cells. The effect depends on local conditions, panel angle, rainfall and how evenly residue is distributed.

We inspect first, document what we find and avoid promising a fixed energy gain. Cleaning is maintenance, not a performance guarantee.
